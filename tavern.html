Trail Stop No. 3

Richardson's Tavern

Exchange Street, Millis, MA 02054 · Established c. 1770

Richardson's Tavern once served as a waypoint for travelers and a gathering place for townspeople during the era of the American Revolution. Taverns like this were the social and political hubs of colonial New England, where news was shared and town matters debated over a hearth. Local lore connects the tavern to militia musters and the comings and goings of the early republic. A historic marker notes its place in the story of the trail.
