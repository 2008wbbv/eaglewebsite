Trail Stop No. 7

Ellice School

Ellice, Millis, MA 02054 · Established 19th century

The Ellice School represents the era of the one-room schoolhouse, when education in Millis and the surrounding mill villages was delivered close to home. Children of mill workers and farmers alike learned their letters within its walls. Such schools were the foundation of civic life and literacy in 19th-century New England. The building survives as a record of how the growing town invested in its youngest residents.
