Trail Stop No. 2

John Partridge House

Village Street, Millis, MA 02054 · Established c. 1714

The John Partridge House is among the oldest surviving dwellings in Millis, built in the decades after Medway was granted its town charter in 1713. The Partridge family were early pioneers who settled near the Charles River for its fertile land and fresh water. The home's timber frame and center chimney are characteristic of early colonial New England construction. It stands today as a private residence and a quiet witness to three centuries of town history.
