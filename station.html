Trail Stop No. 6

Train Station

Main Street, Millis, MA 02054 · Established 1870s

The Millis Train Station was vital to the town's prosperity, allowing locally manufactured goods to be transported to Boston and beyond. The arrival of the railroad helped East Medway thrive on its own and supported the case for separating from West Medway in 1885. The town's namesake, Lansing Millis, was himself a railroad man. The station stands as a symbol of the transportation network that powered the young town's economy.
