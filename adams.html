Trail Stop No. 10

Peter Adams House

Main Street, Millis, MA 02054 · Established c. 1750

The Peter Adams House is a fine example of the early homesteads built by the families who established East Medway. Its location near the heart of the village placed it close to the meeting house and the routes that connected the growing community. The home has weathered the transformation of the town from farming hamlet to manufacturing center to the modern town of Millis. It endures as a private residence and a landmark on the historic trail.
