Trail Stop No. 8

Oak Grove Farm

Oak Grove, Millis, MA 02054 · Established 18th century

Oak Grove Farm reflects the agricultural roots of Millis, established when the fertile land along the Charles River was prized for raising animals and growing crops. Even as mills and manufacturing transformed the local economy, farms like this one sustained the community with food and open land. The farm illustrates the balance between agriculture and industry that defined the town. Its fields remain part of the rural character residents treasure today.
