Trail Stop No. 5

Rockville Chapel

Rockville, Millis, MA 02054 · Established 1846

Rockville Chapel served the mill village of Rockville, one of the small manufacturing communities that grew along the Charles River during the Industrial Revolution. As textile and machine shops drew workers to the area, a local house of worship became essential to the neighborhood. The modest chapel reflects the close-knit character of these riverside mill settlements. It has been preserved as a reminder of Millis's industrial heritage.
