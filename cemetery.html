Trail Stop No. 4

Prospect Hill Cemetery

Prospect Street, Millis, MA 02054 · Established 1714

Prospect Hill Cemetery holds the graves of many of the founding families of East Medway and Millis, with stones dating to the early 18th century. Walking its rows is like reading the town's genealogy carved in slate and marble. Veterans of conflicts from King Philip's War through the modern era rest here. The hilltop setting offers both a place of remembrance and a view over the community these settlers built.
